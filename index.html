LOCATION 01

The Outer Gates

Warm up with identities. Each solved challenge grants Aura and an item type.

+5

Torch of Pythagoras

Simplify sin²(x) + cos²(x).

Reward: Clarity
Recall the Pythagorean Identity.
+5

Shield of Reciprocals

Express sec(x) and csc(x) in terms of cosine and sine.

Reward: Knowledge
Their names tell you which reciprocal identity family to use.
+10

Armor of Angles

Simplify tan(x) · cot(x).

Reward: Insight
tan and cot are reciprocals.
+10

Lens of Symmetry

Simplify cos(−x) sec(−x) tan(−x).

Reward: Reflection
cos and sec are even; tan is odd.
LOCATION TRIAL

Open the Outer Gates

Given x = Rcosθ and y = Rsinθ, explain why x² + y² = R² for every angle θ.

LOCATION 02

The Inner Sanctum

Now the maze tests sum/difference and angle identities.

+15

Scroll of Sum

Find the exact value of sin(13π/12).

Reward: Knowledge
13π/12 = π + π/12, and the angle is in Quadrant III.
+15

Compass of Clarity

Simplify sin(3π/4 − x).

Reward: Clarity
Use sin(a−b)=sin a cos b−cos a sin b. Remember cos(3π/4) is negative.
+20

Amulet of Transformation

Simplify cos(x + π/2).

Reward: Reflection
Apply cos(a+b)=cos a cos b−sin a sin b.
+20

Pendant of Double Angles

Prove the double-angle relationship from the activity by transforming one side into the other.

Reward: Insight
Rewrite cot and tan using sine and cosine, combine the fractions, then look for cos²x−sin²x and 2sinx cosx.
LOCATION TRIAL

Break the Sanctum's Defense

Express cos(2θ) using only cos(θ), then explain what the expression tells you about the horizontal force when the angle doubles.

LOCATION 03

The Heart of the Labyrinth

The highest-value challenges live here. Choose carefully.

+30

Beast of Double Angles

Prove cot(x) sin(2x) = 1 + cos(2x).

Reward: Insight
Use cot x = cos x/sin x and sin 2x = 2sin x cos x.
+30

Wheel of Truth

For h(t)=20+15sin((π/10)t), identify amplitude, midline, and period.

Reward: Reflection
Period = 2π/B.
+35

Chalice of Triple Angles

Derive sin(3x)=3sin(x)−4sin³(x).

Reward: Clarity
Use sin(2x+x), then substitute sin2x and cos2x identities.
+35

Ring of Secant and Cosecant

Determine the period of f(x)=sec(x)+csc(x).

Reward: Knowledge
Ask for the smallest shift that leaves both sec and csc unchanged simultaneously.
